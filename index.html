✦ ABOUT ME

a little about myself

My journey began in Psychology & Communication Sciences, where I was drawn to the intricate intersection of human behaviour and technology. That curiosity carried me into a Master's in Human–Computer Interaction, where I dug deeper into the symbiotic relationship between people and tech.

Now, in UX design, that background lets me approach problems with a holistic perspective. Lately I'm focused on bringing AI into both the products I design and the way I design them — setting up new tools and growing with them to prototype faster, fail early, and validate with users sooner.

Away from the screen, I find clarity scaling rock walls, hiking, and surfing.

Nadja.

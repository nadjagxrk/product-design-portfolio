✦ WHY IT MATTERED

Every repeat question was time not spent winning clients.

Nect verifies identities for other businesses, so its customers are teams — not individuals. When those teams got stuck, they opened tickets, and the same handful of questions came back again and again.

That backlog didn't just frustrate clients waiting on answers. It pulled Sales and Customer Success away from onboarding new business — the work that actually grows the company. Fixing support wasn't a nice-to-have; it was a growth lever.

✦ THE PROBLEM

Where support kept breaking down.

Repetitive tickets
The same questions flooded Sales and Support, over and over.
Tech-heavy answers
Explanations were too technical — customers didn't understand them.
Existing support flow
No client autonomy
Clients had no direct, self-serve way to find answers or track progress.
Scattered knowledge
No single source of truth, so answers were inconsistent and hard to find.

✦ KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS

The calls that shaped the portal.

Decision 01

A portal, not just a help center

The research was clear: FAQs alone wouldn't cut it. So the scope grew into a self-service portal — minimal at launch, built to expand into onboarding, analytics and account management.

Portal overview
Decision 02

A customisable dashboard

Different roles need different things first. After login, users shape their own home from a set of widgets — so the portal fits the person, not the other way around.

Customisable dashboard
Decision 03

Onboarding as a guided checklist

A go-live checklist breaks setup into digestible, checkable steps with an ever-present progress bar — and links straight to the help center or developer manual when someone gets stuck.

Go-live checklist
✦ THE JOURNEY

How it came together.

01 · Discover
Focus group, stakeholder and customer interviews, affinity maps and a Python analysis of the ticket system — to find where support really broke down.
02 · Ideate
User stories built a shared mental model and defined the MVP, and an information architecture mapped the portal's structure end to end.
03 · Design
Wireframes for every section grew into content-rich, high-fidelity screens: login, dashboard, onboarding checklist and an embedded developer manual.
04 · Test
A clickable InVision prototype, tested and refined through feedback loops with fellow designers and the project manager — with real user testing lined up next.

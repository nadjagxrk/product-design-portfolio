✦ WHY IT MATTERED

UX is changing — and that's a chance, not a threat.

AI is reshaping our profession. Rather than treat that as a threat, I see a chance: with the right skills and a design system that's genuinely shared, we can rethink who does what — and free designers to spend their time where it matters most.

For a company scaling from a bookkeeping package into a total solution across ventures, that means moving faster and staying consistent without simply hiring more designers. Tools alone won't get you there — strategy, shared tooling and an enabled team will. So I set out to give it direction.

✦ A NEW WAY OF WORKING

Less busywork. More craft.

The goal was never to replace design work — it’s to hand the mundane, repetitive tasks to AI so the team spends its time where it counts: research, the details, and the calls that need judgement. Along the way it lets the wider business move without waiting on a designer for every small change.

The mundane → to AI

Small, recurring design tasks and design-system checks run through the Snelstart skills instead of by hand.

Designers → freed for depth

That reclaimed time goes straight back into research, edge cases and the craft details that make an experience feel right.

The business → unblocked

Product owners resolve smaller, repetitive design issues themselves with the skills — a designer brackets the flow where judgement matters.

EMERGING
Design → into the system

Enabling designers to make small component changes in the design system directly — shipped as PRs, approved by devs — closing the gap between design and code.

The result: less time on repetitive fixes, more on research and detail — and a business that no longer waits on design for every small change.

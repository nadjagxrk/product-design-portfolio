✦ WHY IT MATTERED

UX is changing — and that's a chance, not a threat.

AI is reshaping our profession. Rather than treat that as a threat, I see a chance: with the right skills and a design system that's genuinely shared, we can rethink who does what — and free designers to spend their time where it matters most.

For a company scaling from a bookkeeping package into a total solution across ventures, that means moving faster and staying consistent without simply hiring more designers. Tools alone won't get you there — strategy, shared tooling and an enabled team will. So I set out to give it direction.

✦ A NEW WAY OF WORKING

POs fix the small stuff — with UX in the loop where it counts.

By communicating the design system widely and packaging it into Claude skills, product owners can now resolve smaller, repetitive design issues themselves — with a UXer at the start and end of the flow, where judgement matters most.

01 · Define & spar
UXer + PO
Frame it together
We define the problem together and spar on first ideas — the thinking that genuinely needs a designer.
02 · PO builds it
PO + Claude skills
Resolve the smaller issues
Using the Snelstart design system and the “UX at Snelstart” skills, the PO fixes smaller, repetitive issues themselves — work that would otherwise cost a UXer time.
03 · Skill-assisted review
Claude review skill
Protect the essentials
A dedicated review skill checks the work against general UX principles and, above all, the Snelstart design system.
04 · Review & refine
UXer + PO + team
Finish it together
Designer and PO review together and refine with the team before it ships.

The result: designers spend less time on repetitive fixes and stay available across more projects at once.
